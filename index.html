The Checkpoint

Your order doesn't go straight from a factory to your door. It comes to us first. Every piece gets checked for print quality and sizing, misprints get sent back and argued about, and whatever passes gets repackaged and sent to you with Canada Post. We call this The Checkpoint. It is mostly a table. It is a very serious table.

  1. The window opensYou pre-order. One item per order.
  2. The window closesWe place one big order with the printer.
  3. The CheckpointEvery piece gets inspected by an actual person who cares too much.
  4. Canada PostIt leaves us and comes to you. Canada only.
