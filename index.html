The Checkpoint

Inspection security, added at checkout

Most online brands ship straight from the printer to you and hope for the best. With The Checkpoint, Mayhem doesn't. Your piece gets inspected by hand for print quality and sizing, misprints get pulled and disputed with the printer, and only what passes gets repackaged and sent to you with Canada Post.

The Checkpoint is inspection security, and it's your call. Add the C$5 Checkpoint Inspection at checkout and we open your order and check the size and print before it ships. If we find a problem, we replace it, and we deal with the producer so you don’t have to. It shows up under shipping because that's how our checkout works, but it's technically a separate purchase.

Skip it and your order ships Sealed, as it comes from the producer. If you think there’s a defect or the wrong item, send us photos within 7 days. We’ll file a claim with the producer and pass on their decision. Normal print variation and minor imperfections aren’t defects.
