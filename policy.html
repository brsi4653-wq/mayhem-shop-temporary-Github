Policy

The plain version, with no jokes. Please read it before you pre-order. You can print or save this page.

Sales

All sales final.

  • Everything is made to order. Your pre-order is what gets made.
  • No refunds or exchanges for size choice, print variation, or minor print imperfections. Please check the size guide before you order.
  • If Mayhem made an actual mistake (for example, we sent you the wrong size), contact us. You ship the item back at your own expense and we fix it.
  • One item per order. Size and colour are chosen on the checkout page.

Prices

  • All prices are in Canadian dollars (CAD).
  • Each product page shows an itemized total before you check out: the item, Canada Post shipping, and the optional Checkpoint Inspection.

Shipping

  • Ships to Canada only.
  • Every pre-order is one item. There's no cart, so two items means two separate pre-orders, and each one pays its own shipping.
  • Every order pays Canada Post shipping. The Checkpoint Inspection is optional and added at checkout:
  • After the pre-order window closes, every pre-order goes to the printer. Each piece is made for its order, then shipped with Canada Post.

Checkpoint Inspection

  • The Checkpoint Inspection is optional inspection security, added at checkout for the price shown above. It appears with shipping because of how our checkout works, but it is a separate purchase.
  • Sealed (no inspection): your order ships as it comes from the producer. If you think there’s a defect or the wrong item, send us photos within 7 days. We’ll file a claim with the producer and pass on their decision. If they approve it, you get the fix they approve. Normal print variation and minor imperfections aren’t defects.
  • Checkpoint Inspection (C$5): we open your order and check the size and print before it ships. If we find a problem, we replace it, and we deal with the producer so you don’t have to.

Nothing here limits any rights you have under the law.

Dates

  • Every quotation shows a ships-by date. All dates and times are Halifax time.

Cancellations

  • Because everything is made to order, pre-orders can't be cancelled once placed.
